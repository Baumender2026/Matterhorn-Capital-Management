1 · Investment thesis

At USD 21.28, with a cost of equity of 6.66%, Hormel is priced for only 1.1% dividend growth forever. Yet consensus has adjusted EPS rising from 1.37 to 1.57 by next fiscal year, and the company targets 5–7% operating profit growth [3]. GAAP numbers hide this: trailing EPS of 0.62 carries charges such as a USD 56m loss on the Brazil sale [1], so the stock screens at 34.3x trailing earnings. This is a modest mispricing, not a deep one. Peers trade below Hormel on forward earnings (11.7x against 13.6x), so we value it halfway between the DDM and the comps, for a margin of safety of +7%. The case is a low-beta, conservatively financed company yielding 5.5%, with dated news inside the challenge.

3 · Valuation: dividend discount model
Why this method

Hormel has paid a dividend every quarter since 1928 and raised it 60 years running [2], with no buybacks in FY24–FY25, so dividends are the cash owners receive. We use two stages because earnings are below normal: higher growth while margins recover, then a low terminal rate.

Methods that do not fit

Gordon alone would ignore the recovery or extend it forever. Trailing P/E is distorted by one-off charges. P/B says little after write-downs across the sector. A cash-flow DCF would start from FY25 FCF, the lowest in four years (USD 534m).

7 · Specific issues
Value trap

FY25 FCF did not cover the dividend (payout/FCF 1.18), the main trap risk. Nine-month FY26 operating cash flow: USD 769m [1].

Sustainable growth

Forward ROE 11.0% × 25% retained = 2.8%, above g2 of 2.0%. First-stage 4.0% needs a lower payout (now 75%).

Management quality

CEO John Ghingo, since 26 Oct 2026 [5], is unproven. Discipline so far: debt/equity 0.36, no buybacks, Brazil and whole-bird turkey sold [1].

Activist investment

No route here: the Hormel Foundation holds 46.8% of the shares [6], so outsiders cannot force change.

Upside potential

If FY27 guidance meets the algorithm and the market pays the DDM value: USD 28.06 (+32%). Analyst mean target: USD 25.31 (+19%).

Risks: what would prove us wrong

Q3 volume fell 7.4% [1]; at beta 1 the DDM is USD 14.28. Wrong if FY27 EPS guidance is below FY26 or FCF again misses the dividend.

Model flags: DDM and comparables disagree (the TA's "mixed: check").

A round of patience rewarded

Dear members of the jury,

The fund returned +4.1% in Round 3 against +2.0% for our benchmark, bringing performance since inception to +11.2% versus +5.6%. The excess return came mainly from stock selection in Swiss financials and our rotation into semiconductors.

The round opened with renewed tariff headlines, but the de-risking we completed in Round 2 left us with a beta of 0.71 and 9% in cash. We used the weakness to add to TSMC and Zurich Insurance at prices we had set in advance.

Late in the round UBS reached our price target of CHF 34. In line with our process we trimmed the position from 8.2% to 6.4% rather than exiting, as the capital-return story remains intact.

Our largest detractor was Sika (−6.7%), where construction volumes in China disappointed. We reviewed the thesis at committee and decided to hold at a reduced 3.9% weight.

Looking ahead, we expect the SNB to keep rates on hold, a modestly weaker franc and continued dispersion in technology. The following pages set out our positioning in detail.

Respectfully,
Luca Di Pietro, Portfolio Manager

Outlook for the final evaluation

Macro. We expect Swiss inflation to stay below 1% and the SNB to hold its policy rate. A gradual easing cycle in the US should support global equities, though valuations leave little margin for error.

Positioning. We keep a modest overweight in equities, a defensive Swiss core and the gold hedge. Cash will be redeployed only at pre-agreed entry levels.

Key risks. Renewed trade tensions, a sharp franc appreciation and a reversal in AI-related capital expenditure are the scenarios we stress-test weekly.

Watchlist. Partners Group, Schindler and Alcon are under review for the next committee.

Key takeaway We close the challenge with excess return locked in, risk well inside limits and capital ready to deploy.

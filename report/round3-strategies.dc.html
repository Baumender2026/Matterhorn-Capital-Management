The macro choice (A1)

Of our two candidates, we implement Strategy A, the regime rule, and put the whole macro sleeve in its pick, hedged global bonds (BNDX); Strategy B, country value and momentum, is not traded (part 2). Neither candidate beat its benchmark in the backtest, and we say so.

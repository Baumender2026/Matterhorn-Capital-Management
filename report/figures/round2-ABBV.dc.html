1 · Investment case
Fair value vs price

The two-stage DDM gives USD 353.68, against a price of USD 291.31. The price implies 4.5% dividend growth forever, although Skyrizi and Rinvoq grew 23–24% in Q3 [1]. GAAP EPS of 5.82 hides this: it carries charges for acquired research [1]. Consensus has adjusted EPS rising from 13.98 to 16.19.

Margin of safety and alpha

Margin of safety +21.4%, alpha +22.9% a year (IR 1.02), assuming the gap closes within one year.

Catalyst

Medicare negotiated price for Botox · By 30 Nov (CMS). Botox is in the third negotiation cycle, and CMS must publish the agreed prices by 30 Nov 2026 [4]. Botox had USD 1.1bn of Medicare spending [4]. A known price, effective in 2028, removes an overhang.

FDA decision on Rinvoq in vitiligo · About 3 Dec (ours). Filed on 3 Feb 2026 [3]; a standard 10-month review points to early December. No date is published. It would be the first systemic treatment for vitiligo [3], supporting the growth in our first stage.

Decision

Long candidate. We buy, although the margin of +21% is below our 25% rule and the comparables give USD 274.70. Growth has returned after Humira, the dividend rises 5.2% in 2027 [1], and the low β limits the risk the stock adds.

2 · Valuation: dividend discount model
Why this method

AbbVie returns cash mainly through dividends, raised every year since 2013, with small buybacks. We use two stages because earnings are rebounding from the loss of Humira's patent, faster than they can grow forever: 22.1% for 4 years, then 3.0%.

Methods that do not fit

Gordon alone gives USD 185.06: it misses the rebound. P/B and ROE are meaningless, as book equity is negative. GAAP P/E is distorted by charges for acquired research [1]. Peers such as Eli Lilly grow at other rates, so the comparables are only a double check.

6 · Specific issues
Main risk: a low β and a high terminal value

Bloomberg's 60-month β of 0.52, far above the 0.20 of our own regression on SPY, gives k of 7.16%, and the terminal value is 88% of the value. A β above about 0.71, or g1 below about 16%, would put the value below the price. Humira sales fell 31% in Q3 [1].

Upside: sustainable growth and management

Skyrizi and Rinvoq already replace Humira, and the 2026 EPS guide rose to USD 13.95–13.99 [1]. CEO Rob Michael, in charge since 2024, keeps raising the dividend while buying research. No activist route at this size.

Model flags: DDM and comparables disagree ("mixed: check"); IR of 1.02 is above Buffett's 0.64: the alpha is probably too optimistic.

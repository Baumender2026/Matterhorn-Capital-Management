1 · Investment case
Fair value vs price

The two-stage DDM gives USD 233.53, against a price of USD 186.64, 23% below the 52-week high. The market prices a slowdown in AI spending, yet Q2 revenue doubled to USD 96.2bn and the company guides USD 108bn for Q3, without China [1]. At 11.7x forward earnings, against 20.6x for peers, the leader trades below the group.

Margin of safety and alpha

Margin of safety +25.1%, alpha +28.2% a year (IR 0.63), assuming the gap closes within one year.

Catalyst

Q3 FY2027 results and Q4 guidance · 17 Nov (company). Set by the company for 17 Nov, after the close [2]; the quarter ended on 25 Oct. A quarter at or above the USD 108bn guide, with Rubin in full production [1], would show that demand is not slowing and move the price towards fair value.

Decision

Long candidate. We buy: the margin of +25% meets our 25% rule, and the comparables (USD 254.34) and analysts (USD 323.79) see more. A dated report can close the gap. Its high residual risk keeps the Treynor-Black weight small.

2 · Valuation: dividend discount model
Why this method

NVIDIA returns cash mostly through buybacks: USD 39.0bn in the first half against USD 6.3bn of dividends [1]. So we discount dividends plus buybacks (cheat sheet p. 2). Two stages, because growth is far above what any company can keep forever: 50.0% for 7 years, then 3.0%.

Methods that do not fit

Gordon alone gives USD 23.56: one rate cannot fit a company growing this fast. P/B (19.68x) says little, as its value is in chip designs and software, not on the balance sheet. Peers are smaller and differently built, so the comparables are only a double check.

6 · Specific issues
Main risk: AI spending and the inputs

Revenue rests on the capex of a few cloud and AI customers: a cut would hit growth fast, and China is out of the guide [1]. g1 is per-share growth and the payout includes buybacks, so they count twice: the DDM is an upper bound on these inputs.

Upside: sustainable growth and management

The quarterly dividend is now USD 0.25 [1], but D0 counts the last 12 months, so the DDM understates it. At β = 1 the DDM gives USD 387.95, and analysts' target is USD 323.79. Founder-CEO Jensen Huang has led it since 1993; no activist route at this size.

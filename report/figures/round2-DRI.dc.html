1 · Investment case
Fair value vs price

The two-stage DDM gives USD 263.32, against a price of USD 226.35: the price implies only 2.8% growth forever. Darden reaffirmed FY2027 EPS of USD 11.10–11.35, 4–7% above FY2026 [1]. The market reads a slow Olive Garden quarter as a trend; management blames the World Cup, and traffic improved into September [2].

Margin of safety and alpha

Margin of safety +16.3%, alpha +17.7% a year (IR 0.95), assuming the gap closes within one year.

Catalyst

Q2 FY2027 results and outlook · 18 Dec (company). Set on the Q1 call for Friday 18 Dec, before the open [2]; the quarter ends in late November. Olive Garden traffic back after the World Cup and a kept EPS guide would show the slowdown was one-off, and move the price towards fair value.

Decision

Long candidate. We buy, although the margin of +16% is below our 25% rule: the comparables (USD 279.60) see more, and the Q2 results fall inside the challenge. A low-β stock that adds restaurants to our chip, food and drug names.

2 · Valuation: dividend discount model
Why this method

Darden returns most of its cash: USD 693m of dividends and USD 672m of buybacks in FY2026 [3], so we discount dividends plus buybacks (cheat sheet p. 2). Two stages: 8.6% for 2 years, from 75–80 new restaurants a year [3] and buybacks, then 3.0%.

Methods that do not fit

Gordon alone gives USD 237.67: it misses the near-term growth. P/B (12.40x) says little, as buybacks have shrunk the equity. McDonald's and Yum franchise most of their restaurants, while Darden runs its own, so margins differ and the comparables are only a double check.

6 · Specific issues
Main risk: Olive Garden and the payout

Olive Garden, over 40% of sales, grew same-restaurant sales by only 1% in Q1 [1]. If traffic stays weak, buybacks, which take the payout above FCF, would be cut first. On dividends alone, the DDM gives about USD 137, well below the price.

Upside: sustainable growth and management

LongHorn has grown same-restaurant sales for 22 quarters in a row, by 6.8% in Q1 [2], and Darden keeps each brand's unit growth below 10% a year [2]. CEO Rick Cardenas raised the dividend by 8% in June, with a new USD 1.5bn buyback programme [3].

Model flags: IR of 0.95 is above Buffett's 0.64: the alpha is probably too optimistic.

01 — Investment philosophy

Diversify across risk, not just across assets.

01
ObjectiveHighest Sharpe over the 1-month T-bill, in all four regimes.
02
50 / 25 / 25 risk budgetEquity gets half the risk; bonds and real assets a quarter each.
03
Risk, not capitalA dollar in equity moves the portfolio about 2.6 times as much as one in bonds.
